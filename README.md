# Lifelines

Turn the people in your life into a short animated video. Every colored line is one person. Those who drift apart fade off to the side. Those who pass away rise into your sky as stars, and at the end they form a constellation of your family.

Everything runs in one file, `index.html`, in the browser. There is no server and no build step. Your people are saved only in your own browser.

## Use it

Open `index.html` in Chrome or Edge, or publish the repo with GitHub Pages.

1. Replace the example family with your own people: name, relationship, and the years they were born, joined, left or died.
2. Press ▶ to preview, or drag the slider through time.
3. Press **Record video**, wait for the story to play through, then **Save video** (MP4 or WebM, 9:16).

## What it shows

- **Family by color.** Parents, grandparents, partner, children and friends each have their own color, which you can change.
- **Children grow out of both parents' lines.** Pick the other parent under *Child with*.
- **Stars and memory threads.** Everyone who dies becomes a star, including people who left your life earlier. Faint threads link each star to the family still living.
- **Whose story.** The same family can be told from anyone's point of view: your partner's, your child's, or your parents'.
- **The future is imagined.** Years after today are drawn as dashed lines, and the age fades out, so the video never says when the story will end.
- **Pacing and captions.** Time slows at big moments (births, meetings, partings, deaths) and speeds through quiet years, with a short caption for each moment.
- **Closing words.** Choose from several closing lines or write your own.
- **Music.** Generated ambient music with a soft chime for every new star.

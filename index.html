People in your story example

An example family. Replace it with your own people. Click a person to edit them. The list runs top to bottom = lines left to right; drag ⠿ to reorder.

  • Joined: the year they came into your life (empty = there from the start).
  • Left: the year your paths parted. Their line drifts away.
  • Died: they rise into the sky as a star.
  • A child's line grows out of both parents' lines. Pick the other parent under Child with.
  • Stars connect by kinship: children to both parents, partners to each other.
  • Whose story (under the preview) tells the same family from anyone's point of view. Use real names so the labels work for everyone, and add birth years.
